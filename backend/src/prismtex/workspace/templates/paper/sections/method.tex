\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

We model the quantity of interest as the mean of $n$ observations
$x_1, \dots, x_n \in \mathbb{R}$:
\begin{equation}
  \bar{x} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i .
  \label{eq:mean}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:mean} is used for every measurement in Section~\ref{sec:results}.
